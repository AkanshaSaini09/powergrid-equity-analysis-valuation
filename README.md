# Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd. – Equity Analysis & Business Valuation
The valuation analysis triangulates PowerGrid's intrinsic value using DDM, DCF, EVA, FCFF, H-Model and Relative Valuation.
## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive equity analysis and business valuation of Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd. The analysis evaluates the company's financial performance, industry position, business fundamentals, intrinsic value and investment attractiveness.

## Objective

The objective of this project is to determine whether Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd. is fairly valued, undervalued or overvalued by analysing its financial performance, industry outlook, risk profile and intrinsic value using multiple valuation methodologies.

## Economic, Industry & Company Analysis

The project uses the EIC (Economic, Industry and Company) framework to evaluate:

- India's economic backdrop and key macroeconomic variables
- Power transmission industry dynamics
- Porter's Five Forces
- PowerGrid's competitive position
- SWOT analysis
- Growth opportunities and key business risks

## Financial Analysis

The financial analysis covers FY2019–FY2025 and evaluates:

- Revenue and operating income
- EBITDA and EBITDA margin
- Profit Before Tax
- Net Profit
- EPS and DPS
- Return on Equity
- Debt-to-Equity ratio
- Interest coverage
- Dividend payout
- Operating cash flows

## Valuation Methodologies

Multiple valuation techniques were used to estimate PowerGrid's intrinsic value:

- Dividend Discount Model (DDM)
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)
- Economic Value Added (EVA)
- Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)
- H-Model
- Relative Valuation

The analysis also includes WACC and CAPM calculations to determine the appropriate discount rate.

## Key Findings

PowerGrid demonstrated strong operating performance, high EBITDA margins, strong operating cash flows and an improving debt-to-equity ratio over the period analysed.

The valuation analysis produced a weighted fair value range of approximately ₹370–₹410 per share, with a target price of ₹420–₹460 per share.

## Investment Recommendation

**Rating: BUY**

**Target Price: ₹420–₹460**

**Investment Horizon: 12–18 months**

The recommendation is supported by PowerGrid's regulated business model, stable cash flows, strong market position, improving leverage and long-term growth opportunities arising from India's transmission infrastructure expansion.

## Key Risks

The analysis considers several risks, including:

- Valuation sensitivity to WACC and growth assumptions
- CERC tariff revision risk
- Competition from private transmission players
- DISCOM payment delays
- Interest rate risk
- Infrastructure project execution risk

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd. represents a fundamentally strong and stable infrastructure company. Its regulated business model, strong cash flows, dominant position in power transmission and long-term infrastructure opportunities support its investment attractiveness.

Based on the valuation analysis and growth outlook, the project concludes with a **BUY** recommendation.
